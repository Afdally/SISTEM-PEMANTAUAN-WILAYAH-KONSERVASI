# Admin Login and Sidebar Refinement

## Approved direction

- Keep the marine glass identity, but use conventional administration typography.
- Keep the SIMEKA brand inside the login card.
- Replace the two-part login heading with one heading: **Login**.
- Make email and password the primary login method; keep Google as a secondary option.
- Keep authentication explicitly in design mode until the backend is connected.
- Keep the desktop sidebar fixed from below the header to the bottom of the viewport.
- Reduce the Dashboard heading to a normal application-page scale.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop: fixed sidebar, sticky header, content scrolls independently in the document.
- Mobile and tablet: no sidebar; retain the existing bottom navigation.
- Login fields and actions remain full-width with at least 44 px touch targets.
